For example, the <span class="tex-font-style-tt">mex</span> of the multiset $$$[0, 2, 3]$$$ is $$$1$$$, while the <span class="tex-font-style-tt">mex</span> of the multiset $$$[1, 2, 1]$$$ is $$$0$$$.</p><p>More formally, on the step $$$m$$$, when Ildar already has an array $$$a_1, a_2, \ldots, a_{m-1}$$$, he chooses some subset of indices $$$1 \leq i_1  \lt  i_2  \lt  \ldots  \lt  i_k  \lt  m$$$ (possibly, empty), where $$$0 \leq k  \lt  m$$$, and appends the $$$mex(a_{i_1}, a_{i_2}, \ldots a_{i_k})$$$ to the end of the array.</p><p>After performing all the steps Ildar thinks that he might have made a mistake somewhere. He asks you to determine for a given array $$$a_1, a_2, \ldots, a_n$$$ the minimum step $$$t$$$ such that he has definitely made a mistake on at least one of the steps $$$1, 2, \ldots, t$$$, or
